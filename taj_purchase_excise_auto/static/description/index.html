Auto Excise Duty on Purchases

Volume-based duty calculated for you, on every purchase order. Free.

If your goods attract a duty charged per unit of volume, someone on your team is probably calculating it by hand on every purchase order. This module does the math instead: switch on the toggle, and a duty line is added automatically from the total volume of the order and the rate you configured. Change the order and the line recalculates. Switch the toggle off and it disappears.

Originally built for the Nigerian excise regime, but the product and rate are fully configurable, so it fits any per-volume levy anywhere.

Configurable in Settings

Pick your excise product and set the rate per volume unit in Purchase Settings. No code, no hardcoded values.

Smart exclusions

Free items and products without volume are left out of the calculation, so promos and services never inflate your duty.

Keeps itself current

Add lines, change quantities, adjust prices: the duty line recalculates on save, in draft or after confirmation.

Landed cost aware

Use the excise product as a landed cost split by volume and free items are excluded from the distribution, so paid stock carries the duty correctly.

Setup

  1. Create a service product for the duty (e.g. "Excise Duty"), purchasable, and mark it as a landed cost with split method By Volume if you use landed costs.
  2. In Purchase Settings, select it as the Excise Duty Product and set your rate per volume unit.
  3. On any purchase order, flip the "Auto Excise Duty" toggle. Done.
